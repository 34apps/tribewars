## 1. The short version

- **Flames you have not spent:** ask for a refund within **14 days** of buying them, with one button in your account page. Your money goes back to the way you paid.

## 2. What you can buy

- **Flames:** an in-game currency, sold in packs for euros. Every price is shown in euros as well as in Flames.

Flames are never sold for troops, resources, protection time, battle strength, Renown or score.

## 3. Your 14-day right of withdrawal, and why it ends

In the EU, when you buy something online you normally have 14 days to change your mind. For **digital content** that is handed over at once, the law lets that right end **if you clearly ask for the content to be delivered right away and confirm that you lose the right of withdrawal** once it has been delivered.

At checkout we show a tick box that you must tick before you can pay:

> I want my Flames now. I understand that I lose my 14-day right of withdrawal for Flames that I use.

We store the time, the version of the terms and your tick with the purchase.

**What we do beyond the law:** we do not hold you to the strict version of that rule. Even after you ticked the box, you can still withdraw from the purchase within 14 days **as long as you have not spent any Flames from it** (section 4). Once you spend Flames, the right of withdrawal for those Flames is gone.

When you spend Flames on Premium or on a look, the game shows you a short confirmation with the euro price and tells you that the service starts at once and that this ends the right of withdrawal for it. You confirm it before the Flames are spent.

## 4. Refund of unspent Flames (within 14 days)

1. Go to your account page, choose the purchase, press **"Refund unspent Flames"**. Or write to **[contact email]** from the email address on your account.
2. You may ask for a refund of a purchase if **none** of the Flames from that purchase has been spent. Flames are counted as spent **oldest first**: if you bought two packs and spent some Flames, the spending is counted against the older pack first. A pack that is only partly spent cannot be refunded in part, unless the law says otherwise.
3. The refund is for the **full price you paid**, including the VAT in it. We do not keep a fee. The refund goes through Stripe to the card or payment method you used.
4. The Flames of that purchase are removed from your account at the same time.
5. We pay the refund **without delay and at the latest within 14 days** after your request. Your bank may need a few more days to show it.
6. Time limit: the 14 days start the day after you bought the Flames.

If you press the button by mistake, nothing is lost but the Flames: you can buy again.

## 5. Spent Flames

- **Spent Flames are not refunded.** That includes Flames spent on Premium or on a look. We do not pay out the value of Flames once they have been used.
- **Looks:** a look you bought stays yours for as long as the account exists.
- **Exceptions:** we may make a goodwill or fault exception, for example a bug in the shop or the game took Flames or Premium without delivering, you were charged twice, or the game was unavailable for a long stretch of your Premium time. In that case we give you the Flames or the Premium days back (or, where the law requires it, the money). Every exception is written down.
- **Premium in a world that ends early:** if we end a world before your Premium runs out, we give you the unused Premium days back as Flames (the number of Flames follows from 150 Flames for 30 days, rounded down per day).
- Your rights under consumer law for faulty digital content stay as they are.

## 6. Bank disputes (chargebacks) and reversed payments

A **chargeback** is when you ask your bank or card company to take a payment back instead of asking us. Please ask us first: the refund in section 4 is faster, free and has no consequences for your account.

If a payment is disputed or reversed without us having agreed to a refund:

1. **The Flames of that purchase are removed from your account at once** (we are told by Stripe within seconds). If you already spent them, your Flames balance goes **below zero**.
2. **Paid features are locked** while the balance is below zero or a dispute fee is open: you cannot spend Flames on Premium or looks, and Premium that is running may be paused. You can still play the free game.
3. **Fees:** you owe us the fee that Stripe charges us for the dispute (shown to you in the notice we send, **[amount from Stripe]**) **plus a handling fee of 4 EUR** for our work in answering it. The handling fee is a flat amount for our real costs; it is not a penalty.
4. **How to settle:** we send you a notice by email and show it in the account page. You settle it by paying the open amount through a normal Stripe checkout ("Settle account"), and Flames you buy after that first fill up the minus. When the balance is zero and the fees are paid, the lock is lifted.
5. **If the dispute ends in your favour** (you withdraw it, or the bank decides for you): the Flames are given back and any fee we asked for the dispute is refunded, but only if the money really comes back to us. If the money does not come back, steps 1 to 4 stay.
6. **If the card was used without the owner's permission** (the real card owner did not make the purchase): we remove the Flames from the account that received them, and the card owner is never charged our fees. If your own card was stolen and you tell us, please say so in the notice reply and we will look at it before charging you anything.
7. **A second chargeback** on the same account means we **ban the account from paying**. **Payment fraud** (using a card that is not yours, many accounts used to cash in on disputes) means we **close the account**.
8. We may give Stripe the evidence it asks for in a dispute: the purchase record, your consent tick, login times and what the Flames were spent on. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

## 7. If the game closes for good

If we shut Clanflame down for good (not a single world: the whole game), we will:

- tell you by email and in the game **at least 30 days before**;
- **pay back the money for your unspent Flames.** The amount is what you paid for those Flames. Spending is counted oldest first, so the Flames you still have are counted from your **newest** purchases;
- pay it to the payment method you used, or, if that is no longer possible, to a bank account you give us.

Premium days that are still running at that point are paid back in the same way, by the price of the Flames they cost. Looks are not paid back.

## 9. Closed accounts, bans and changed terms

- **You close your account:** unspent Flames bought in the last 14 days can be refunded as in section 4. Other unspent Flames are lost when the account is deleted, so spend them first. We remind you of this before you confirm.
- **We close your account for breaking the terms:** unspent Flames and running Premium are forfeited. If the closing was a mistake, we undo it and give everything back. Where the law says that we must pay back money for Flames you have not used, we do.
- **We close an account for age reasons or by mistake:** we refund what the person (or the parent) paid.
- **We end your account for no fault of yours** (for example an inactive account closed after 24 months with a warning): unspent Flames are refunded on request if you answer in time.
- **We change the terms against you:** you may close the account within the 14-day notice and get unspent Flames back.

## 2. Recruit

Ladder: every building at 25 / 50 / 75 / 100% of its max level (rounded). Income = engine production for those levels (Gold Mine included at the same pct, otherwise gold would be 0). Pop room = Longhouse cap minus the pop of ALL buildings at that pct. The player spends 100% of income on one army type; stock starts at one full Storehouse of wood/food/iron (no gold). Money days include that stock. One building trains one unit at a time. Binding = which of money / the one training building is slower (the room caps the size, the queue only limits how far ahead one can order).

# Siege units: balance experiments (real engine, fixed seeds)

Unit prices (valued wood + food + iron + 10 x gold): Log Ram 620 (pop 3), Boulder Sling 720 (pop 2), Raider 160, Pikeman 105, Shieldbearer 145. Points to lower a level: L1 8, L3 12, L6 18, L10 26, L15 36, L20 46. In a decisive win (attack = 2 x defence) 64% of an army comes back, so about 157% of the survivors must be sent.

## (c) Attacks needed against a fortified village, with and without siege

Each wave: the attacker sends Raiders + Log Rams so that attack = 2 x the defence the engine reports (Wall as it stands before the battle), wins, the garrison dies. Between waves the defender brings the garrison back to 50% of the original. Rams are sized to take 3 Wall levels from the starting level (survivors / 0.64). No siege = the same waves with no Rams (the Wall never drops).

## (d) Does siege make defending pointless?

### (d1) Siege acts only after a WON battle: the same attack at different strengths

Attacker: 60 Log Rams + 20 Boulder Slings (picking the Banner Hall) + enough Raiders for the ratio. Defender: Wall 10, Banner Hall 3, Market 5, 250 Pikemen + 150 Shieldbearers. 9 seeds per row (luck +-10%).

| attack / defence | battles won | buildings lowered when LOST | levels lowered when WON (avg) | attacker loses (value) | defender troops lost (avg) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.60 | 0 / 9 | 0 of 9 | - | 88,720 | 182 |
| 0.90 | 0 / 9 | 0 of 9 | - | 107,920 | 335 |
| 1.00 | 4 / 9 | 0 of 5 | 0.0 | 111,451 | 380 |
| 1.10 | 7 / 9 | 0 of 2 | 0.0 | 107,684 | 399 |
| 1.25 | 9 / 9 | - | 0.0 | 96,058 | 400 |
| 1.50 | 9 / 9 | - | 0.6 | 82,207 | 400 |
| 2.00 | 9 / 9 | - | 1.0 | 65,178 | 400 |
| 3.00 | 9 / 9 | - | 2.1 | 48,171 | 400 |

A defence that holds loses no building level, ever. Even a narrow win does almost nothing: the winner loses (defence/attack)^1.5 of its army, siege units included, so too few survive to lower a level; the engine only gets to "levels lowered" with a clear win.

## Invariants

- OK: per-attack cap: Rams at most 3 levels
- OK: per-attack cap: Slings at most 2 levels
- OK: Wall levels are cheap to rebuild vs the Rams that lower them (rebuild < attacker capital)
- OK: against a garrisoned village siege lowers the next wave's defence by under 40%
- OK: a lost battle never lowers a building
- OK: a real garrison + Wall needs a much bigger army even with siege (at least 5x the empty village)
- OK: a siege column is at least 1.8x slower than Raiders over 20 tiles

All invariants hold.

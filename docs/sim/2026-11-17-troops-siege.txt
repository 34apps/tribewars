# Firecart: balance experiments (real engine, fixed seeds)

Unit prices (valued wood + food + iron + 10 x gold): Firecart 368 (pop 3), Raider 85, Pikeman 56, Shieldbearer 75. Points to lower a level: L1 8, L3 12, L6 18, L10 26, L15 36, L25 56. In a decisive win (attack = 2 x defence) 63% of an army comes back, so about 157% of the survivors must be sent.

## (c) Attacks needed against a fortified village, with and without Firecarts

Each wave: the attacker sends Raiders + Firecarts so that attack = 2 x the defence the engine reports (Wall as it stands before the battle, after the during-the-battle hit), wins, the garrison dies. Between waves the defender brings the garrison back to 50% of the original. Firecarts are sized to take 2 Wall levels after the battle from the starting level (survivors / 0.63). No siege = the same waves with no Firecarts (the Wall never drops).

## Invariants

- OK: per-step cap: at most 2 levels
- OK: the Wall takes at most 2 x 2 levels when it is hit in both steps
- OK: no pick: the Wall is the target
- OK: the Farm and the Longhouse never drop below 1
- OK: Wall levels are not a bargain to knock down (rebuild < 1.5 x attacker capital)
- OK: against a garrisoned village siege lowers the next wave's defence by under 40%
- OK: a lost battle never lowers more than the two steps may lower in one attack
- OK: a real garrison + Wall needs a much bigger army even with siege (at least 5x the empty village)
- OK: a siege column is at least 1.8x slower than Raiders over 20 tiles

All invariants hold.

Step 11: homepage + first steps

1 · Homepage

  • Someone who is not signed in sees this page instead of the bare sign-in box (same look as the mockup).
  • It lives at the lobby address, the one front door for all worlds. "Play free" opens Create account, "Sign in" opens Sign in.
  • The world list at the bottom shows the real open worlds (name, speed, players, age). Joining still needs an account.

2 · First steps (8 small tasks)

  • A new player gets 8 short tasks: Lumber Camp 2, Farm 2, Town Hall 2, Barracks 2, train 5 Raiders, raid an abandoned village, open the report, join or found a tribe.
  • Each task ticks itself as soon as you start it (or if you already did it). You tap Claim and get a few resources: about 1,200 of each plus 100 gold for all 8 together.
  • The card sits on the village screen with Mags: top of the side column on a computer, a slim bar on the phone. It can be hidden, and it goes away after the last task.

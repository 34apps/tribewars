Mail · choosing who to write to

The game has no friends list. What it does have: the players you have mailed with, your tribe members (with role) and a player search. A mail still goes to one player.
A · Picker column
A “Who to write to” column between the list and the form: search any player, recent conversations, your tribe with roles and who is online. Click a name and it fills To.
B · Choose band + Contacts (NEW)
Under To, one band: search with suggestions as you type, then Contacts | Recent | Tribe side by side. Contacts (players you pin) is a NEW feature, marked NEW, for you to decide.

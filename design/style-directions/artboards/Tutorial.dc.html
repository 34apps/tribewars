Mags's tasks · the tutorial

Ten short jobs that teach by doing. Every step lights one control, Mags says exactly what to tap, and each finished task pays resources. Skippable any time.

After Skip: one pill, Village screen only. Settings can turn Mags back on too.

Skip

  • Skip from Mags's card or the tasks card. One confirm: "Skip Mags's tasks? You keep every reward you've claimed."
  • Spotlights and the card go; tasks still tick off quietly.
  • Tapping outside a spotlight never blocks: it fades, and the task waits with Show me.

Resume

  • Resume picks up at the first unfinished task.
  • Done already? It completes on its own. Nobody repeats work.
  • Rewards wait until claimed and never expire. Storehouse full: "claim later".
  • Timers show a countdown; Mags suggests something useful meanwhile.
